Who this policy covers

This policy explains how jodi ("we," "us," "the app") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the jodi mobile app or website. By creating an account, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Account details: email address, password (stored as a salted hash — we never store or can see your plain-text password), name, and birthdate (used to verify you're 18 or older; not shown publicly beyond your age)
  • Profile content: city, prompt answers, interests, languages, faith, family closeness, views on kids, profile photos, and an optional voice introduction clip
  • Messages you send to matches, and reports you file against other users

Information generated by your use of the app:

  • Who you like, pass on, or send a rose to, and who you match with
  • Read receipts and typing indicators, visible only to the other person in an active match
  • Basic technical logs (IP address, device/browser type, timestamps) collected automatically by our hosting infrastructure for security and abuse prevention

We do not collect your precise location (GPS). We do not currently use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

How we use your information

  • To operate the app: authenticate you, show you compatible profiles, deliver matches and messages in real time, and compute your profile strength
  • To keep the app safe: review reports, enforce blocks, and act on violations of our community standards
  • To communicate with you about your account (e.g. security notices)

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share your profile, messages, or contact details with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Who can see what

  • Your profile (name, age, city, photos, prompts, voice intro) is visible to other users the matching system shows you
  • Messages are visible only to you and the person you matched with
  • Reports and blocks are visible only to jodi, for safety review — never to the person you reported or blocked

Service providers

We use third-party infrastructure providers (for hosting, storage, and delivering the app) who process data on our behalf under contract, solely to provide the service — they don't use your information for their own purposes.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. You can permanently delete your account at any time from within the app (Me → delete my account). Deleting your account immediately and permanently removes your profile, photos, voice intro, prompts, swipe history, matches, and messages — this cannot be undone and we cannot recover it for you afterward.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can exercise most of these directly in the app (profile editing, account deletion); for anything else, contact us using the details below.

Children's privacy

jodi is restricted to users 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that an underage person has created an account, we will delete it.

Security

Passwords are hashed, not stored in plain text. Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

International users

Your information may be processed in a country other than the one you live in. By using jodi, you consent to this transfer.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the effective date above and, where required, notify you in the app.
